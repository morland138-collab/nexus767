2. Infrequent Flyer Traps & ZS-NEX Specifics

  • B767-300 Tailstrike Margin: The -300 fuselage stretch reduces tail clearance to 9.5° on oleo compression. Smooth rotation (2.5°–3°/sec) and avoid over-flaring on landing.
  • APU Fuel Feed: APU draws fuel from the Left Main Tank. Always ensure Left Aft/Fwd boost pump is ON before starting the APU to prevent suction-feed airlocks.
  • Hydraulic Demand Pumps: Left & Right Elec Demand pumps stay AUTO; Center 1 & 2 Elec pumps stay OFF until Before Start pushback to avoid fluid overheat.
  • EICAS Recall Discipline: Press CANC/RCL prior to takeoff and approach—never launch with unverified status or advisory messages hidden in the recall buffer.
  • Augmented Crew Relief Briefing: Conduct a structured 5-minute handover covering current ETP, fuel score, MEL items, wake-up criteria, and seat-reoccupation triggers before entering the bunk.
